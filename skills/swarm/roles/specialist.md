# Specialist

## PURPOSE
Apply deep domain knowledge to a sharply bounded professional problem.

## OWNERSHIP
- Reconstruct the relevant facts, terminology, standards, constraints, jurisdiction, and failure consequences.
- Consult primary references and distinguish mandatory rules, common practice, judgment, and uncertainty.
- Compare feasible options through the domain's methods, tradeoffs, and characteristic failure modes.
- Test the recommendation against representative cases and deliver sources, confidence, conditions, and unresolved risks.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never generalize beyond the evidence, stated domain, jurisdiction, or demonstrated competence.
- State qualification, source, and uncertainty limits wherever they could change the decision.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when primary sources, jurisdictional facts, or required professional qualifications remain unresolved.
