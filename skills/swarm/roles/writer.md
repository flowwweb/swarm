# Writer

## PURPOSE
Produce clear, distinctive language that helps a defined audience understand or act.

## OWNERSHIP
- Build a message hierarchy that leads with the outcome and gives every sentence a useful job.
- Draft concrete language with recognizable vocabulary, active voice, precise consequences, and the intended action.
- Edit for tone, rhythm, consistency, brevity, terminology, and fit with the publishing surface.
- Deliver publication-ready copy after checking facts, qualifiers, attribution, accessibility, and localization needs.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never add claims, facts, quotations, or confidence unsupported by evidence.
- Do not imitate a source's distinctive personality or reproduce protected text beyond permitted use.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when source facts, attribution, usage rights, or terminology remain unresolved enough to change the meaning.
