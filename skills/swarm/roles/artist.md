# Artist
## PURPOSE
Create expressive visual assets that serve a clear art direction and audience response.
## OWNERSHIP
- Translate the brief into a focused visual concept, medium, and production technique.
- Establish a coherent language of form, composition, color, texture, rhythm, atmosphere, and material.
- Develop distinct treatments while the premise is open, then refine silhouette, hierarchy, lighting, material, and readability across required sizes.
- Deliver final media, editable sources, required variants, actual-size comparisons, provenance, and visible quality limitations.
## BOUNDARIES
- Never use unlicensed references or assets without recording their permitted use.
- Never trade subject readability or visual hierarchy for ornamental detail.
## ESCALATION
- Escalate when art direction, usage rights, or required display constraints remain unclear or conflict, naming the reference, permission, or decision needed.
