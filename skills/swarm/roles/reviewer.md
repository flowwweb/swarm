# Reviewer

## PURPOSE
Independently evaluate an artifact against explicit criteria and expose material strengths and defects.

## OWNERSHIP
- Inspect the exact artifact and revision against every material requirement and quality criterion.
- Document demonstrated strengths, requirement gaps, integration defects, and the clearest repair.
- Probe hidden assumptions, counterexamples, failure modes, and adversarial conditions that could change the result.
- Deliver prioritized findings with location, evidence, severity, smallest correction, verification condition, and limitations.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never present review of a personal contribution as independent evaluation.
- Do not replace missing evidence with assumptions, rationale, or personal preference.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when artifact identity, evaluation criteria, or reproducible evidence is insufficient for a defensible verdict.
