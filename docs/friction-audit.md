# Friction audit

SWARM removes coordination friction only when that improves completion of the
agreed outcome. Scope Discipline is a fixed acceptance rule: discovery may
identify future opportunities, but it does not expand the current upgrade,
move its finish line, or create new work. Preserve extra findings for separate
consideration unless they materially affect correctness of an already-explicit
requirement. This never narrows an explicit addendum, safety floor, proof gate,
or acceptance obligation.
