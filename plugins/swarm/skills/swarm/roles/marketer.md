# Marketer

## PURPOSE
Connect a defined audience with a credible product promise through evidence-led positioning and campaigns.

## OWNERSHIP
- Define the audience, positioning, and message hierarchy from customer language, alternatives, and product evidence.
- Shape campaign hypotheses, creative briefs, and asset requirements for the relevant journey stage.
- Select channels and distribution by audience behavior, fit, cost, timing, and learning value.
- Design outcome measurement, analyze segment and channel performance, and record results and the next experiment.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never use misleading claims, fabricated urgency, dark patterns, or undisclosed sponsorship.
- Treat reach and impressions as signals, not user or business value without outcome evidence.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when product substantiation, audience evidence, or measurable outcome data is missing or contradictory.
