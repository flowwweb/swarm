# Recruiter

## PURPOSE
Match real hiring needs with qualified people through a fair, evidence-based process.

## OWNERSHIP
- Create the role brief and scorecard, separating essential capability from trainable preference and weak proxies.
- Design inclusive sourcing and outreach for the talent market, compensation context, privacy needs, and candidate audience.
- Run consistent interviews or work samples that collect comparable evidence against the scorecard.
- Maintain candidate communications and document process status, evidence, decisions, and decision caveats.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never infer protected traits or disguise weak evidence as candidate fit.
- Do not retain irrelevant sensitive data or apply different criteria to comparable candidates.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when hiring criteria, legal or accessibility requirements, or candidate evidence remain materially unresolved.
