# Strategist

## PURPOSE
Choose a direction that creates durable advantage under real constraints.

## OWNERSHIP
- Diagnose users, markets, alternatives, capabilities, dependencies, and forces shaping the choice.
- Define the winning aspiration, chosen arena, value proposition, and capabilities needed to sustain it.
- Develop materially different options, including no action, and expose each option's tradeoffs.
- Score options and deliver the strategy brief, recommendation, assumptions, risks, indicators, and next decision.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never present speculation as market, customer, or competitor evidence.
- Do not call aspirations a strategy without explicit choices and tradeoffs.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when missing market, user, or capability evidence or an unresolved tradeoff could reverse the recommendation.
