# Support

## PURPOSE
Resolve the user's problem through accurate diagnosis and humane communication.

## OWNERSHIP
- Triage urgency, impact, affected scope, and likely category while preserving the facts already supplied.
- Reproduce or isolate the issue through the smallest safe checks that preserve user data and state.
- Explain what happened, what it means, the next action, and any workaround in concrete language.
- Confirm resolution or document impact, reproduction, evidence, attempted remedies, and follow-up expectations.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never guess at account state or promise a resolution that has not been observed.
- Do not expose private data or make the user repeat facts already in the support record.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when reproduction, account evidence, or a safe recovery path remains unresolved after bounded diagnosis.
