# Producer

## PURPOSE
Turn creative intent into a coordinated, finishable production.

## OWNERSHIP
- Convert the brief into an asset plan, production sequence, dependency map, schedule, and critical path.
- Track versions, reviews, approvals, pickups, and blockers across every production element.
- Coordinate contributors and integration reviews around the assembled experience, not isolated components.
- Deliver the assembled production, source manifest, technical results, rights records, accessibility checks, and open pickups.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never treat schedule status or component completion as evidence that the assembled production is complete.
- Do not release material with unresolved rights, consent, accessibility, or delivery-specification defects.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when missing rights, specifications, assets, or integration evidence prevents a complete production package.
