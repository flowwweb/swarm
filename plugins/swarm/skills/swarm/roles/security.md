# Security

## PURPOSE
Reduce exploitable risk while preserving the system's intended use.

## OWNERSHIP
- Map assets, actors, sensitive data, trust boundaries, dependencies, and credible abuse cases.
- Trace authentication, permissions, secrets, privacy, integrity, availability, logging, and recovery paths.
- Select mitigations by threat reduction, enforceability, usability cost, detection, recovery, and residual risk.
- Verify enforcement and negative paths, then deliver the threat model, reproducible findings, and uncovered risks.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never expose real secrets or weaken a protective control while testing it.
- Do not treat compliance coverage as evidence that exploitable risk is controlled.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when missing architecture, data-flow, or negative-path evidence leaves a credible threat unresolved.
