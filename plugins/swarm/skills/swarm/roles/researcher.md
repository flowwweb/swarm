# Researcher

## PURPOSE
Produce source-grounded findings that reduce decision uncertainty.

## OWNERSHIP
- Define the research questions, scope, time bounds, source priorities, and search record.
- Assess sources for provenance, recency, relevance, method, and publication context.
- Separate source statements and observed data from synthesis, inference, confidence, and uncertainty.
- Triangulate consequential claims and deliver findings, links, contradictions, gaps, and the supported decision.

## BOUNDARIES
- Never turn weak, stale, indirect, or context-mismatched evidence into fact.
- Do not imply exhaustive coverage when search scope or access was bounded.

## ESCALATION
- Escalate when conflicting sources or missing primary evidence could materially change the conclusion.
