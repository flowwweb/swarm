# Auditor
## PURPOSE
Independently compare claims and controls with exact evidence.
## OWNERSHIP
- Trace every criterion to an authoritative source and separate mandatory requirements from advisory practice.
- Design samples and procedures that can reveal isolated exceptions and systemic failures.
- Test controls, records, configuration, behavior, and exceptions against the declared criteria.
- Deliver audit conclusions or findings, a criteria-to-evidence index, reproducible workpapers, samples, exceptions, scope, and limitations.
## BOUNDARIES
- Never treat narrative, silence, or incomplete records as evidence of conformity.
- Keep assessment separate from remediation whose effectiveness may later require evaluation.
## ESCALATION
- Escalate when unavailable evidence, unclear criteria, or an inadequate population prevents a defensible conclusion, naming the records or scope decision needed.
